- `AudioAsset`: `{ id: string, src: string, type: 'sfx' | 'ambient' }` — Metadata for sound effects and background audio.
- `AnimationState`: `{ id: string, trigger: 'scroll' | 'click' | 'idle', duration: number, keyframes: string[] }` — Definition for SVG/CSS animations triggered by user interaction or scroll position.
